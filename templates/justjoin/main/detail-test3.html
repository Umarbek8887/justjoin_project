About the Role

Privacy is not just a compliance checkbox at Remitly. It's a core tenet of our vision and product philosophy; a necessary driver of our Global Privacy program.

What You'll Do

  • Lead privacy program management across technical and product teams globally
  • Drive implementation of Privacy by Design principles in product development
  • Manage Data Protection Impact Assessments (DPIAs) and privacy risk reviews
  • Collaborate with engineering, legal, and compliance to ensure GDPR and global privacy compliance
  • Build and maintain privacy documentation, policies, and data mapping
  • Oversee vendor privacy assessments and third-party data processing agreements
  • Partner with security teams on incident response involving personal data

What You'll Bring

  • 5+ years of experience in privacy program management or data protection
  • Deep knowledge of GDPR, CCPA, and global privacy regulations
  • Technical background or ability to engage effectively with engineering teams
  • Experience with OneTrust or similar privacy management platforms
  • CIPP/E or equivalent privacy certification preferred
  • Excellent communication skills in English (C1 minimum)
